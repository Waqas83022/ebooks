# A small request {.unnumbered}

If this book helped you take back even one evening, finish one task you'd been avoiding, or put your phone down at one dinner, please leave an honest review wherever you bought it. Reviews are how other readers, the student staring at the same page for an hour and the parent trying to fit a dream into the gaps, find a book like this.

And if you know someone who keeps promising themselves they'll start tomorrow, pass it on.

# About the author {.unnumbered}

Muhammad Waqas writes practical books about the everyday struggles that stand between people and the lives they want. His other books include *The Archer and the Wind: How to Keep Going When Hard Work Isn't Enough* and *AI for Barbershop Owners*.

*Focus Is Your Superpower* is built around a question almost everyone has asked themselves at some point: "I know what I should be doing. Why can't I make myself do it?" The answer in these pages draws on research into attention, habits and procrastination, and it is written to be used, not just read.
