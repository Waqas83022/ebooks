## A note on the evidence

Some techniques in this book, such as if-then plans, retrieval practice and spacing, have been tested in hundreds of studies. Others, such as the Pomodoro Technique, the Eisenhower matrix and time blocking, are widely used frameworks with less direct experimental evidence behind them. They're included because many people find them useful and because they put better-tested principles into practice. Where the evidence for a popular idea is weak or mixed, as with the "brain drain" study in Chapter 6 or the claim that habits take 21 days, the chapter says so.
