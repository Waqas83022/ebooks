## Description

KDP's description box accepts a small set of HTML tags. Paste this version. It is your draft from the brief, with the list matched to what the finished book contains.

```html
<p><b>How much of your life is slipping away while you're waiting for the right moment to begin?</b></p>
<p>You have goals. You have ambitions. You know what you want to accomplish.</p>
<p>Yet somehow, your days fill with notifications, endless scrolling, unfinished tasks, overthinking, and promises to start tomorrow.</p>
<p>The problem isn't always a lack of motivation. Often, it's the absence of a practical system for protecting your attention and turning intentions into action.</p>
<p><i>Focus Is Your Superpower</i> is a practical guide to reducing distractions, overcoming procrastination, and making meaningful progress without turning your life into an endless work schedule.</p>
<p><b>Inside this book, you'll discover how to:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Understand the hidden emotional triggers behind procrastination.</li>
<li>Reduce unnecessary screen time and social media distractions, step by step, without giving up your phone.</li>
<li>Bring your mind back when it drifts into daydreams, worry or overthinking.</li>
<li>Improve concentration through structured focus exercises that start where you are.</li>
<li>Use proven planning tools, such as the urgent-important matrix, time blocking and if-then plans, to prioritize what truly matters.</li>
<li>Stop overthinking and make difficult tasks easier to begin.</li>
<li>Build daily routines and habits that support consistent action.</li>
<li>Apply focus techniques to studying, work, business, and family life.</li>
<li>Recover from unproductive days without abandoning your progress.</li>
<li>Follow a practical 30-day challenge to develop your own focus system.</li>
</ul>
<p>With real-life examples, reflection exercises, planning templates, and practical techniques drawn from research on attention and habits, this book goes beyond temporary motivation.</p>
<p>It helps you build a way of working that fits your goals, responsibilities, and energy.</p>
<p>You don't need to control every minute of your life. You need to take control of the moments that matter.</p>
<p>Start reclaiming your attention, and turn the goals you've been postponing into actions you can take today.</p>
```

## Categories

KDP lets you pick up to three categories. The names in KDP's picker change from time to time; choose the nearest match to these:

1. Self-Help > Time Management
2. Self-Help > Personal Transformation (or Self-Help > Motivational)
3. Business & Money > Skills > Time Management

If you want to reach students in particular, an education or study-skills category is a reasonable swap for the third.

## Price

Suggested Kindle price: $4.99 (US). That sits inside KDP's 70% royalty band ($2.99 to $9.99), and the EPUB is under 0.5 MB, so the delivery fee is very small. Check the exact fee KDP shows on the pricing page.

A launch price of $2.99 for the first week or two can help early reviews, then move to $4.99.

## Paperback

If you publish a paperback, choose a 6 x 9 inch trim and a black-and-white interior on cream or white paper. The proof PDF in `output/` is for reading and review; KDP needs an interior PDF with its own margin settings and a full wraparound cover (front, spine and back) sized to the final page count. KDP's cover calculator gives the exact size.

## Before you publish

1. Read the whole book once in KDP's previewer after upload, on both a Kindle e-reader view and a phone view.
2. Personalize the "About the author" page and, if you like, the dedication (see `author-notes.md`).
3. Check that the title is not already used by another book in a way that would confuse buyers. Search Amazon for it.
4. Run EPUBCheck on the EPUB if you want a formal validation report (see `author-notes.md`).
5. Upload the EPUB and cover, fill the forms using this kit, and publish only when the previewer looks right.
