# A small request {.unnumbered}

If this book saved you an hour, filled a quiet Tuesday or stopped one bad reply from going out at midnight, please leave an honest review wherever you bought it. Reviews are how other shop owners find a book like this, the same way they are how new clients find your shop.

And if you know a barber who is still answering the phone with the clippers running, pass it on.

# About the author {.unnumbered}

Muhammad Waqas writes practical guides for owners of small, local businesses who want to use new tools without losing what makes their business personal. He is interested in the unglamorous side of running a shop: the missed call, the empty hour, the review that needs a reply. *AI for Barbershop Owners* grew out of a simple question: what would a busy barber actually use, between clients, on a phone?
