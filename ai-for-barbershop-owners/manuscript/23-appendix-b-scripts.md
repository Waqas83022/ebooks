# Appendix B: Scripts and messages to adapt {.unnumbered}

These are finished examples, written for Cedar Street and edited by a person. Swap in your own facts, read them aloud, and change anything that does not sound like you. Placeholders are in [square brackets].

Marketing messages (anything that promotes openings, offers or the shop to someone who has not just contacted you) go only to clients who have agreed to receive them, with opt-out wording where required. Service messages (replies, reminders about a booked appointment, missed-call texts) do not carry promotions.

## Phone and messages

**Missed-call text-back**

> Hi, this is Cedar Street Barbers. Sorry we missed your call, we're probably mid-cut. You can book any time here: [booking link]. Or reply to this text and we'll get back to you between clients.

**Voicemail greeting**

> You've reached Cedar Street Barbers. We're probably cutting right now. The quickest way to book is the link we'll text you in a minute. We're open Tuesday to Friday 10 to 7 and Saturday 8 to 5. If you have a question, leave a message and we'll call you back.

**After-hours auto-reply**

> We're closed for the night. You can book any time here: [booking link]. If you have a question, we'll answer from 10 a.m. Cedar Street Barbers

**"How much is a cut?"**

> A standard cut is $35, skin fades are $45, and cut and beard is $50. Full menu and booking: [booking link]

**"Do you have anything today?"**

> Here's what's open today, it's live: [booking link]. If you don't see a time that works, tell me roughly when and I'll check.

**Offering and holding a slot**

> [Barber] has [time] today for a [service]. Want it? I'll hold it for 15 minutes.

**If they don't reply in time**

> No worries, I've released that slot. Here's what's open if you want another time: [booking link]

**Barber is fully booked**

> [Barber] is booked up until [day]. I can add you to the waitlist for an earlier spot, or [other barber] has [time] if you'd like that instead.

**Question about a chair renter**

> Keisha books her own clients and sets her own prices. You can see her menu and openings here: [Keisha's link]

**Running late (client texts)**

> Thanks for the heads-up. We've got a 10-minute grace period. If you'll be later than that, we can do a [shorter service] in the time left, or move you to [next slot]. Which works better?

**Barber running behind (shop texts)**

> Hi [first name], [barber] is running about [minutes] minutes behind today. You're welcome to come at [new time], or [other barber] can take you at your original time. Just reply and let us know.

## In the shop

**Fully booked walk-in**

> Sorry, we're booked up until [time]. Here's a card, you can book any open time in a few seconds. Tuesday and Wednesday afternoons are usually easy to get.

**Honest wait estimate**

> There are [number] ahead of you with [barber], so probably [range]. [Other barber] might be free sooner, around [time]. Or I can book you a time later today.

**Rebooking at the mirror**

> Want me to put you in for the same time in [interval]? I can see what's open.

**If they say no**

> No problem. I'll send you a reminder when you're due, if that's OK with you.

**Asking for a review at checkout**

> Glad you like it. If you get a minute later, we'd really appreciate an honest review on Google. The code's on this card.

**Asking permission for a photo**

> Mind if I get a photo of the fade for the shop's Instagram? I can leave your face out if you'd rather. And it's totally fine to say no.

**Recommending a product**

> I used the matte clay today. It's what keeps it from going flat by the afternoon. It's on the shelf if you want to try it.

## Reminders and follow-ups

**Day-before reminder**

> Hi [first name], see you tomorrow at [time] with [barber] for your [service]. Need to move it? Use this link up to 12 hours before: [reschedule link]. Cedar Street Barbers

**Two-hour reminder**

> See you at [time] with [barber], [first name]. Running late? Reply here and we'll sort it out. Cedar Street Barbers

**Thank-you and review request**

> Thanks for coming in today, [first name]. If you'd like to share how your visit went, here's our Google review link: [review link]. Honest feedback helps us, good or bad. Cedar Street Barbers

**No-show recovery**

> Hey [first name], we missed you today at [time]. No worries, life happens. Want to rebook? Here's the link: [booking link]

**"You're due" reminder (marketing; opted-in clients only)**

> Hey [first name], it's [barber] at Cedar Street. It's been about [interval] since your last [service]. Want to grab a time? [booking link] [Opt-out wording]

**Win-back (opted-in clients only)**

> Hey [first name], it's Dre at Cedar Street. It's been a while, hope all's good. If you're due for a cut, I've got openings this week: [booking link]. [Opt-out wording]

**Quiet-slot message to an overdue regular (opted-in)**

> Hey [first name], [barber] at Cedar Street has a few weekday openings this week if you're due for a cut. Standard cut is $35. See what's still free: [booking link]. [Opt-out wording]

**Waitlist opening**

> Hey [first name], the [day] opening you asked about just came up. [Barber] has [verified slot] for a [service]. You can grab it here: [booking link]. The calendar shows if it's still open.

**Public availability post**

> Got half an hour Tuesday or Wednesday? Tomás has a few openings between noon and 4 at Cedar Street. Standard cuts are $35. Pick a time: [booking link]

## Reviews and complaints

**Reply to a positive review (three variations)**

> Thanks, [name]. Tomás will be glad the fade held up for the wedding. See you next time. Dre

> Appreciate you taking the time, [name]. Keeping the line-up sharp is what Dre lives for. See you in a few weeks.

> Glad your son was comfortable for his first cut. That's the best part of the job. Thanks for the review.

**Reply to a negative review**

> I'm sorry about your visit. [What went wrong, in one plain sentence, owning our part.] We've [what we changed]. I'd like to hear more and make it right. Please call me at [shop phone]. [Name], owner

**Reply to a review you believe is not genuine**

> We can't find a visit that matches this, and we'd like to understand what happened. Please call me at [shop phone]. [Name], owner

**In-person complaint, first words**

> Thanks for telling me. I want to get this right. Can you tell me what happened? I'll make sure Dre hears about it today, and he'll call you by tomorrow.

## Referrals and partners

**Referral card**

> Bring a friend to Cedar Street. They get $10 off their first visit. You get $10 off your next one after they've been in. Just have them give your name when they book.

**Referral text (opted-in clients)**

> [First name], if a friend needs a barber, send them our way: they get $10 off their first cut and you get $10 off your next. They just give your name when booking: [booking link]. [Opt-out wording]

**Partnership pitch**

> Hi [name], it's Dre from Cedar Street Barbers up the road. Would you be up for a simple one-month trial? I'll keep your cards at my counter and mention you when clients ask, and you put ours at your front desk with a link that has your code on it. At the end of the month we check whether either of us got anyone. No pressure either way.

**Partner front-desk sheet**

> Cedar Street Barbers, 412 Cedar Street (parking behind, entrance on Pine Alley). Skin fades, beard work and textured hair. Standard cut $35, skin fade $45, cut and beard $50. Book here with your code: [partner link]
