## Description

KDP's description box accepts a small set of HTML tags. Paste this version:

```html
<p><b>You can cut. Your clients come back. So why is Tuesday afternoon empty, the phone ringing out mid-fade, and the review from last month still waiting for a reply?</b></p>
<p>Most of the work that decides whether a barbershop is busy happens away from the chair: answering calls and DMs, reminding clients, posting cuts, replying to reviews, filling quiet slots, keeping the books straight. It doesn't pay, it never ends, and it lands on the owner.</p>
<p><i>AI for Barbershop Owners</i> shows you how to hand much of that work to an AI assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, for less than the price of one haircut a month, while you and your team stay in charge of prices, bookings and every message that goes out. No tech skills needed. Every chapter ends with something you can do before your next shift.</p>
<p>Inside:</p>
<ul>
<li>The one-page "shop brief" that stops AI from inventing prices and promising slots you don't have</li>
<li>A missed-call text that recovers callers who would otherwise book down the road</li>
<li>A one-week routine for filling quiet weekday afternoons at full price, with the arithmetic for when a discount is worth it</li>
<li>Reminders, rebooking at the mirror and win-back messages that keep regulars coming, and the texting consent rules to follow</li>
<li>How to fix your Google Business Profile and earn honest reviews without breaking Google's rules or the FTC's 2024 fake-review rule</li>
<li>Calm replies to one-star reviews, written when you're not calm</li>
<li>How to test a paid ad with a fixed budget and know whether it actually brought in clients</li>
<li>Pricing by the chair hour: why a popular service can earn less per hour than a basic cut</li>

<li>A 30-day plan: one baseline, one problem, one change at a time</li>
</ul>
<p>Plus 100 copy-and-paste prompts, ready-to-adapt scripts, 15 worksheets, 15 diagrams, and three worked cases: a solo barber with quiet afternoons, a busy shop fighting no-shows, and a chair-rental shop sharing one brand.</p>
<p>Written for solo barbers, shops with employees and shops that rent chairs. Use it on your phone between clients.</p>
```

## Price

Suggested Kindle price: $7.99 (US). That sits inside KDP's 70% royalty band ($2.99 to $9.99). The EPUB is about 1.2 MB, so the 70% band's delivery fee is small. Check the exact fee KDP shows on the pricing page, because it is calculated from the converted file.

A launch price of $2.99 for the first week or two can help early reviews, then move to $7.99.

## Before you publish

1. Read the whole book once on a Kindle or the Kindle app, using KDP's previewer after upload.
2. Personalize the "About the author" page (see `author-notes.md`).
3. Check the tools and prices named in Chapter 1 and Chapter 8 are still current.
4. Run EPUBCheck on the EPUB (see `author-notes.md`) if you want a formal validation report.
5. Upload the EPUB and cover, fill the forms using this kit, and order nothing until the previewer looks right.
