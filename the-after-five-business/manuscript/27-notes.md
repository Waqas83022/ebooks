# Notes and sources {.notes}

Laws, tax thresholds, platform fees and software terms change. The figures below were checked in 2026; confirm current rules before relying on them.

## Introduction

Side-hustle figures: Bankrate, "Fewer Americans have a side hustle in 2025," press release and survey report, 2025. The survey of 2,616 US adults was conducted by YouGov on June 2 to 4, 2025. It found 27 percent of adults had a side hustle (36 percent in 2024, 39 percent in 2023) and that side hustlers earned an average of $885 a month. Press coverage of the same survey reported a median of about $200 a month.

The "jagged frontier" study is described in the notes to Chapter 1.

## Chapter 1

Shakked Noy and Whitney Zhang, "Experimental evidence on the productivity effects of generative artificial intelligence," *Science* 381, no. 6654 (2023): 187–192. The preregistered experiment had 453 college-educated professionals. Time taken fell by about 40 percent and graded quality rose by about 18 percent; the weakest writers gained the most. The authors note that output was not fact-checked.

Erik Brynjolfsson, Danielle Li and Lindsey R. Raymond, "Generative AI at Work," *Quarterly Journal of Economics* (2025); first circulated as a working paper in 2023. The study covers more than 5,000 customer-support agents. Resolutions per hour rose about 14 percent on average and about 34 percent for novice and low-skilled workers, with minimal gains for the most experienced.

Fabrizio Dell'Acqua, Edward McFowland III, Ethan Mollick, Hila Lifshitz-Assaf, Katherine Kellogg, Saran Rajendran, Lisa Krayer, François Candelon and Karim R. Lakhani, "Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier: Field Experimental Evidence of the Effects of AI on Knowledge Worker Productivity and Quality," Harvard Business School Working Paper 24-013 (2023), later published in *Organization Science*. The experiment involved 758 BCG consultants. For tasks inside the frontier, consultants using GPT-4 completed about 12.2 percent more tasks and worked about 25.1 percent faster, with substantially higher rated quality. On a task designed to fall outside the frontier, consultants using AI were about 19 percentage points less likely to produce a correct solution than the control group. Reported figures differ slightly between versions of the paper.

Xiang Hui, Oren Reshef and Luofeng Zhou, "The Short-Term Effects of Generative Artificial Intelligence on Employment: Evidence from an Online Labor Market," *Organization Science* (2024). After ChatGPT's release, freelancers in writing-related occupations saw monthly jobs fall about 2 percent and monthly earnings fall about 5.2 percent relative to less exposed occupations; image-generating tools had similar effects on image work.

## Chapter 2

Peter M. Gollwitzer and Paschal Sheeran, "Implementation intentions and goal achievement: A meta-analysis of effects and processes," *Advances in Experimental Social Psychology* 38 (2006): 69–119. The meta-analysis covered 94 independent studies and found a medium-to-large effect (d = 0.65) of if-then plans on goal attainment.

## Chapter 3

California Labor Code section 2870. States with similar employee-invention statutes include Delaware, Illinois, Kansas, Minnesota, North Carolina and Washington; Nevada and Utah have their own variants; New York Labor Law section 203-f took effect in September 2023. Coverage and wording differ by state.

Federal Trade Commission, "Federal Trade Commission Files to Accede to Vacatur of Non-Compete Clause Rule," press release, September 2025. The rule had been set aside in August 2024 by the US District Court for the Northern District of Texas in *Ryan, LLC v. FTC*. The Commission voted 3–1 to dismiss its appeals.

US work visas: guidance from university international offices and immigration practitioners generally treats freelancing or self-employment outside the sponsoring employer as unauthorized employment for H-1B holders. UK Skilled Worker supplementary-work rules were changed in April 2024 and again in July 2025. Take individual advice.

FINRA Rule 3270 (Outside Business Activities of Registered Persons) requires registered persons to give prior written notice to their member firm. FINRA has proposed replacing Rules 3270 and 3280 with a single Rule 3290; check the current rulebook.

Mei and Ravi are composites.

## Chapter 5

Federal Trade Commission, Trade Regulation Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials, 16 CFR Part 465, in force since October 2024. It prohibits fake reviews and testimonials, including reviews generated by AI that misrepresent the reviewer's experience.

Pilot prices in this chapter are the author's estimates for a first, limited project in a high-cost English-speaking market. They are not survey data.

## Chapter 7

Samsung's 2023 restriction on generative AI tools followed reports that engineers had entered internal source code into ChatGPT. The policy covered company-owned computers, tablets and phones and internal networks, as reported by Bloomberg and widely covered in May 2023.

## Chapter 9

*Mata v. Avianca, Inc.*, No. 22-cv-1461 (S.D.N.Y.). On June 22, 2023, Judge P. Kevin Castel imposed a $5,000 sanction on two lawyers and their firm for submitting a brief citing non-existent cases generated by ChatGPT.

Damien Charlotin maintains a public database of court decisions involving AI-hallucinated content. Press reports in June 2026 put the total at about 1,600 cases from more than 30 countries.

## Chapter 10

*Moffatt v. Air Canada*, 2024 BCCRT 149 (Civil Resolution Tribunal of British Columbia, February 14, 2024). The tribunal found Air Canada liable for negligent misrepresentation by its website chatbot and awarded C$650.88 in damages, plus C$36.14 in pre-judgment interest and C$125 in tribunal fees.

## Chapter 11

Fiverr's standard seller fee is 20 percent of the order value. Upwork moved to a variable freelancer service fee, set per contract and ranging from 0 to 15 percent, in 2025. Check each platform's current fee pages.

## Chapter 12

New York State Freelance Isn't Free Act (General Business Law Article 44-A), in force statewide from 2024, following New York City's 2017 law of the same name. It requires a written contract for freelance work of $800 or more (including work for the same hiring party over 120 days) and payment by the contract date or within 30 days of completion. The Illinois Freelance Worker Protection Act took effect on July 1, 2024.

## Chapter 13

Freelancers Union, survey findings published in 2015 in support of New York City's freelancer-payment legislation: 71 percent of freelancers reported having trouble collecting payment at some point.

## Chapter 15

US 1099 thresholds: the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (2025) raised the Form 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC reporting threshold from $600 to $2,000 for payments made from 2026, indexed for inflation from 2027, and restored the Form 1099-K threshold for payment platforms to more than $20,000 and more than 200 transactions. Reporting thresholds do not change what is taxable.

US self-employment tax and estimated tax: IRS guidance for the self-employed (Publication 334 and Form 1040-ES instructions). Self-employment tax is 15.3 percent (12.4 percent Social Security up to the annual wage base and 2.9 percent Medicare), applied to 92.35 percent of net earnings, and is generally due once net earnings reach $400. Estimated payments are generally required when you expect to owe $1,000 or more after withholding and credits.

UK: HMRC guidance on the £1,000 trading allowance, Self Assessment registration and Making Tax Digital for Income Tax (mandatory from 6 April 2026 for qualifying income over £50,000, falling to £30,000 from April 2027 and £20,000 from April 2028). Class 4 National Insurance rates for 2025–26 are 6 percent on profits between the lower and upper limits and 2 percent above. Record-keeping: at least five years after the 31 January submission deadline for the relevant tax year.
