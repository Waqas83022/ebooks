# A small request {.request}

If this book helped you, even a little, please consider leaving an honest review on Amazon. A sentence or two about what you tried and what happened is more useful to other readers than any description I could write, and it helps other employed people who wonder whether an evening business is possible find a plan that keeps their job safe.

If you ran a pilot using this book, I would like to hear how it went, whatever you decided at the end.

# About the author {.about}

Muhammad Waqas writes practical nonfiction for people who want to build something of their own without gambling the life they already have. *The After-Five Business* is written for employees who want an honest way to earn extra income from skills they already use every day, with AI tools doing some of the work and their careers kept safe.

Muhammad Waqas is also the author of *The Archer and the Wind: How to Keep Going When Hard Work Isn't Enough*.
