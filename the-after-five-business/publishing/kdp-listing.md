### Plain text version

You have a full-time job, a contract you signed in a hurry, and maybe five spare hours a week. You also have a skill people at work rely on. This book shows you how to turn that skill into a small, paid, AI-assisted service, without putting your job at risk.

The After-Five Business is a practical plan for employees. It skips passive-income promises and starts from what the research on AI at work shows: the tools make drafting, summarizing and sorting fast, and they make confident mistakes. Clients pay for the parts the tools can't do: asking the right questions, checking what's true, fitting the result to a real business and standing behind it.

Inside you will learn how to:

- protect five hours a week with two work sessions, one admin hour and a client cap
- check your contract for outside-work, IP, confidentiality and conflict clauses, and keep a clean line between your employer and your business
- turn one everyday skill into a fixed-scope offer a stranger can understand and buy, using a catalogue of twelve services that fit an evening schedule
- brief any AI assistant with a six-part method, and keep client data out of places it shouldn't go
- follow three complete worked pilots (a process manual, a sourced research brief and an enquiry automation with a person approving every message) with prompts, checks and real time logs
- find your first three buyers through warm introductions, run a 20-minute discovery call and send a one-page proposal
- set a price from your floor, the market and the value to the buyer, and get paid on time
- handle tax, records and insurance basics in the US and the UK
- run a six-week paid pilot and decide, from evidence, whether to repeat, reprice, redesign or retire the offer

The book includes 17 diagrams and a toolkit of worksheets and templates: an employer boundary checklist, offer card, starter AI briefs, outreach messages, proposal template, quality gates, pricing worksheet, time and profit log, and pilot review.

Five hours a week. Ten weeks. One small business that is entirely yours.

## Categories

KDP lets you choose up to three categories. Look for these, or the nearest match in the category picker:

1. Business & Money › Small Business & Entrepreneurship › Home-Based
2. Business & Money › Small Business & Entrepreneurship › Startups (or "Starting a Business")
3. Business & Money › Small Business & Entrepreneurship › Entrepreneurship

A technology shelf (Computers & Technology › Artificial Intelligence) is possible, but the book teaches a business method more than technical AI skills, so it will compete better on the business shelves.

## Price

- Launch at **$6.99** (inside the 70 percent royalty band of $2.99 to $9.99).
- After 30 days and at least ten reviews, test **$7.99** for a month and compare units and royalties.
- Kindle Unlimited (KDP Select) is worth trying for the first 90 days if you don't plan to sell the ebook elsewhere.

## Content and rights questions

- **Language:** English
- **Publishing rights:** I own the copyright and I hold the necessary publishing rights.
- **Primary audience:** no sexually explicit content; reading age 18 and up is reasonable for a business book.
- **AI-generated content:** KDP asks whether the text, images or translations were AI-generated. Answer truthfully. This manuscript was drafted with an AI assistant, so select "Yes" for text, and describe it accurately. The diagrams were drawn as HTML/CSS code and rendered to images, so they are not AI-generated images in the usual sense, but if you edit or regenerate them with an AI tool, say so.
- **DRM:** your choice. Many nonfiction authors leave it off.
