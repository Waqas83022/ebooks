## Description

KDP's description box accepts a small set of HTML tags. Paste this version into the box:

```html
<p><b>You worked hard. You hoped deeply. Perhaps you prayed. And it still went the other way.</b></p>
<p>The exam missed by two marks. The visa refused after three years. The job that went to someone else. The shop on the road the traffic abandoned. The person who chose a different life. If our destiny is already written, why work so hard? If we get what we work for, what is destiny for? And why does luck seem to favor the people who prepare?</p>
<p><i>The Archer and the Wind</i> brings the four forces people build their lives on, hard work, luck, destiny and hope, to the same table. It starts from an image the ancient Stoics used: an archer who does everything she can to aim well, and still cannot control the wind. The bow is your effort. The wind is luck. The written page is destiny. And the breath before release is hope: the moment you act without knowing how it will end.</p>
<p>Inside you will find:</p>
<ul>
<li>Why luck really does seem to favor the hardworking, and the four reasons behind it</li>
<li>Why the best candidate often loses, and what success stories leave out</li>
<li>A 2,000-year-old answer to the question "If the future is fixed, why try?"</li>
<li>How to grieve a future you had already started living in</li>
<li>The kind of hope that finds new roads, and the kind that breaks hearts</li>
<li>How to live through a long wait without letting it break you</li>
<li>How to decide whether to persist, change your method or let go</li>
<li>The archer's card: five questions for any goal, decision or disappointment</li>
</ul>
<p>For everyone who did everything right and still did not get what they wanted. You were not foolish to try.</p>
```

## Price

KDP pays a 70% royalty on Kindle books priced between $2.99 and $9.99 in the main stores. For a debut nonfiction ebook of this length, $4.99 is a common starting price. You can run a launch price of $2.99 for the first week or two and raise it later.

## Before you press Publish

1. Read the proof PDF from start to finish, out loud where you can. Change anything that does not sound like you.
2. Rewrite the About the author section in your own words and add anything you want readers to know. The dedication is also yours to change.
3. Open the EPUB in Amazon's free Kindle Previewer to see it on phone, tablet and Kindle screens.
4. The book does not quote other writers. Every set-off line is original to this book, and what other people said or wrote is told in paraphrase, with sources in the Notes. Song lyrics are not quoted anywhere.
5. Decide whether to enroll in KDP Select (exclusive to Amazon for 90 days, in exchange for Kindle Unlimited reads and promotion tools).
6. After publishing, check the book's page on Amazon and record your categories and ranking on the date you check.
