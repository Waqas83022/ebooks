## Description

KDP's description box accepts a small set of HTML tags. Paste this version into the box:

```html
<p><b>You can do the makeup in your sleep. It's the booking that keeps you up at night.</b></p>
<p>"Hi! Are you free October 10? It's me and maybe four others. We need to be ready before photos. How much would it be?"</p>
<p>Every bridal makeup artist knows that message. It gives you one fact and five questions. Answer too fast and you quote a morning that doesn't fit. Answer too slowly and she books someone else. Then come the deposit that hasn't arrived, the trial notes you can't read in October, the venue that changes its access time and the mother of the bride who decides, nine days out, that she'd love makeup too.</p>
<p><i>AI for Bridal Makeup Artists</i> shows you how to use any AI chat tool, including the free ones, to take the typing, the drafts and the first versions of your schedules off your plate, while you keep every decision that matters. The AI drafts. You approve every price, date and promise before it reaches a client.</p>
<p>One fictional wedding runs through the whole book, from the first DM to the final touch-up, so you can see how a decision in the inbox shows up on the wedding morning.</p>
<p>Inside you'll build:</p>
<ul>
<li>A one-page studio brief that stops AI inventing prices, holds and discounts</li>
<li>Three first replies: date open, date booked, and too uncertain to quote</li>
<li>A five-question intake form and a simple inquiry tracker</li>
<li>Itemized quotes, follow-ups that get answered, and a deposit checklist</li>
<li>A trial recap the bride confirms, and a wedding-day look brief</li>
<li>Solo and two-artist wedding-morning timelines that work backward from the ready-by time and fit around hair</li>
<li>A change request form and three scripts for late changes, so you never overbook yourself</li>
<li>A closeout checklist and a monthly scorecard that shows which inquiries turn into work</li>
</ul>
<p>Every chapter ends with a ten-minute action. Appendix A gathers 17 copy-ready templates and prompts in one place. Appendix B gives you three practice bookings with worked answers. Appendix C shows what to do when an AI draft invents a price, confirms a date too early or schedules a bridesmaid in two chairs at once.</p>
<p>No tech skills needed. No client details in the chat. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and whatever comes next.</p>
<p><b>Let the AI take the typing. Keep the promises for yourself.</b></p>
```

4. Upload the EPUB and preview it in KDP's online previewer on phone, tablet and Kindle e-reader settings. Check the diagrams and the template boxes in particular.
5. Upload the cover JPG and check the thumbnail on the KDP bookshelf.
6. Fill in the description, keywords and categories from this file.
7. Answer the AI content questions.
8. Set the price and territories, then publish.
