# Appendix A: Copy-ready templates {#appendix-a}

Every template from the book, in the order you'll use them. Copy them into your notes app, your booking system or a document you keep on your phone. Replace anything in square brackets with your own details, and keep the placeholders that should stay as placeholders until the moment you send.

## A1. Studio brief

::: template
**STUDIO BRIEF: [Studio name]**

*Version [number]. Last updated [date].*

**About**
[Two or three sentences: who you are, where you work, solo or team, who you serve, what you don't offer.]

**Services and approved rates**
[Service]: $[price]. Includes [what's included]. [Minutes] minutes.
[Repeat for every service, add-on and trial.]

**Timing**
Setup: [minutes], starting when I have access to the actual makeup room.
Buffer before ready-by time: [minutes] for up to [number] services; [minutes] for more.
Maximum per artist per morning: [number] services.
Earliest start: [time]. Earlier starts need my approval and carry [fee].

**Travel (per artist)**
[Zone or distance]: $[fee].
[Zone or distance]: $[fee].
Over [distance]: I decide and quote personally.

**Booking terms**
Minimum spend: [amount and when it applies].
Deposit: [rule]. It is applied to the total.
A date is booked only when the agreement is accepted AND the deposit is verified in my payment system. Until then the date is not held.
Quotes are valid for [number] days.
Balance due [when]. Changes agreed before then are added to the balance. Services added after the balance is paid are paid on the day.
Cancellations, refunds and changes are covered by the agreement. Do not summarize or explain them. Refer the client to the agreement.

**Tone of voice**
[Eight lines or fewer. Length, formality, openings, sign-off, punctuation habits, words you use.]
Never say: [list].

**The AI may**
Use the rates and timings above. Calculate totals from them. Describe what's included. Ask the intake questions. Draft a schedule and label it "proposed." Write follow-ups. Reword my drafts.

**Hand back to me (do not decide)**
Whether a date is available. Any discount, package or price not listed. [Travel limit]. [Early start limit]. Adding a second artist. Anything about refunds, cancellations or exceptions. Confirming that a booking is made, a change is agreed or a payment is received. Anything not covered here.

**Privacy**
I use a lead ID and placeholders such as [Client first name]. Never ask for, and never include, full names, phone numbers, email addresses, venue addresses, photos, health or skin details, or payment details. If you need one of these to finish a draft, leave a bracketed gap.

**When information is missing or contradictory**
Leave a bracketed gap and list the problem under the draft. Never guess a number, date, time, link or policy.
:::

## A2. Pre-send checklist

::: checklist
1. **Date.** Right date, and calendar checked today?
2. **Numbers.** Every price, total and deposit matches my rate sheet and my arithmetic?
3. **Promises.** No hold, discount, time or confirmation I haven't decided?
4. **Placeholders.** Every bracket filled, name spelled correctly?
5. **Voice.** Sounds like me?
6. **Thread.** Right person, right conversation?
:::

## A3. Inquiry form

::: template
**Wedding makeup inquiry**

1. What is your wedding date?
2. Where will you be getting ready, and what time can suppliers get into the room?
3. What time does everyone need to be makeup-ready? Is there an earlier photo, first look or departure time?
4. How many people would like makeup, including you? Are any still undecided?
5. Would you like information about a bridal trial?

Anything else you'd like me to know? (optional)
:::

## A4. First replies

**Date open (DM, under 70 words)**

> Hi [Client first name]! Thanks for getting in touch. [Date] is currently open in my calendar. Could you tell me where you'll be getting ready, what time everyone needs to be ready for photos, and how many people would like makeup, including you? Once I have those details I'll check the timing and send you an itemized quote.

**Date booked (email, under 90 words)**

> Hi [Client first name],
>
> Thank you so much for thinking of me for your wedding. I'm already booked on [date], so I can't take this one, I'm afraid. [Optional: My colleague [name] may be free. You can reach her at [contact].]
>
> Wishing you a wonderful day,\
> [Artist first name]

**Too uncertain to quote (DM, under 80 words)**

> Hi! Congratulations. My current rates are $[bridal rate] for bridal makeup and $[attendant rate] for each bridesmaid or family member, plus travel, which depends on where you're getting ready. Once you have a date, send it over with your venue and how many people would like makeup, and I'll check my calendar and send you an itemized quote.

## A7. Quote prompt

::: prompt
Draft a friendly bridal makeup quote email in 150 words or fewer using only the details below. Use [Client first name] as the greeting. Make each service line and the travel charge easy to see. Show optional services separately. Describe the schedule as proposed. State that the booking is confirmed once the agreement is accepted and the deposit is verified. Leave any missing information in brackets. Flag contradictions separately. Do not invent fees, deadlines, links or policies.

Details: [Date]. [Each service with price and subtotal]. Travel $[fee]. Wedding-day total $[total]. Deposit $[deposit] applied to that total. Balance $[balance], due [when]. Optional [trial or extras] $[price], billed separately if booked. Proposed setup at [time], all makeup ready by [time], subject to [what needs confirming]. Quote valid for [number] days. Use [secure booking link] as a placeholder.
:::

## A8. Follow-up

> Hi [Client first name], I wanted to see whether you had any questions about the makeup quote for [date]. If you're deciding whether to include a trial, I'm happy to send a few dates. If the number of people has changed, let me know and I can update the quote and timings.

**Second and final follow-up**

> Hi [Client first name], just a note that your quote for [date] is valid until [day]. After that I'll need to check availability again before confirming the date. If you have any questions, I'm happy to help.

## A10. Trial recap

::: prompt
Turn my trial notes below into a short recap email for the bride. Use three headings: "What we agreed," "What I'll adjust on the day," and "What I'd like you to confirm." Plain language she'll understand. Keep every product name and decision exactly as written. Don't add products, techniques or claims about longevity. If a note is undecided, put it under "What I'd like you to confirm." If adding services would change the price, say so and use the rates in my brief. Under 200 words. Use [Client first name]. Then, separately, list anything in my notes that affects timing.

[Anonymised trial notes]
:::

## A12. Wedding-morning worksheet

::: checklist
1. **Deadline.** The earliest time anyone must be ready, confirmed with the person who set it.
2. **Buffer.** From your brief, before the deadline.
3. **Services.** Confirmed services and realistic minutes. A separate column for each artist.
4. **Setup.** Unloading, walking in, setting up, counted from access.
5. **Feasibility.** Calculated start compared with access. Hair and photography checked.
:::

## A14. Schedule release checklist

::: checklist
1. **Verify the math.** Every service fits with the buffer intact. Sum checked by hand.
2. **Check the people.** No person or artist in two places at once.
3. **Confirm the handoff.** Room access, hair schedule and deadline confirmed with the people responsible.
4. **Version and date.** On both copies.
5. **Share a simple version.** Chair times, arrival instructions and a contact for lateness. Internal notes stay in the studio copy.
:::

## A16. Closeout checklist

::: checklist
1. **Money.** Total owed matches total received, from payment records. Add-ons included. Associate settled.
2. **Thank-you.** Sent within two days, with one specific detail.
3. **Review.** Requested separately, with a link. No incentives.
4. **Portfolio.** Permission asked separately from people in the photo and from the photographer. Answers recorded.
5. **Records.** Tracker closed. Client files kept or deleted according to your policy. AI chats tidied.
6. **Lessons.** Three lines written. Brief updated if needed.
:::
