# The lines worth keeping {.unnumbered .lines}

*An archer's quiver, gathered in one place: the lines from this book that readers may want to return to, copy down or pass on.*

## The bow: on hard work {.unnumbered}

*Effort changes the odds. It does not sign a contract with the outcome.*

*No farmer has ever believed that plowing guarantees rain. Every farmer plows.*

*A result tells you what happened. It does not tell you what you are worth.*

*Hard work deserves respect, even when it does not receive applause.*

*Hard work is two different things wearing one coat: strain, and aim.*

*Persistence needs feedback as much as it needs courage.*

*A method deserves your loyalty only while it deserves your confidence.*

*Exhaustion is a cost, not a contribution.*

*A fair account of achievement includes the ground the ladder stood on.*

*Your starting point was luck. Your direction is not only luck.*

*Not everything called bad luck is luck. Some of it has an author.*

## The wind: on luck {.unnumbered}

*Work does not summon luck. It builds a place for luck to land.*

*The wind can only carry arrows that have been released.*

*Preparation cannot create the accident. It decides whether the accident becomes an opportunity.*

*Luck often has a human face. Remember to thank it.*

*The drowned do not paint pictures.*

*Every success story has a sample size. Ask who was left outside it.*

*It does not take much wind to decide a contest between archers who are all excellent.*

*Social media is a wall of votive tablets. The drowned do not post.*

*The old man did not say it was good. He said it was not finished.*

*A single result contains wind. A pattern contains you.*

*We call it luck too early. Add two words: so far.*

*Judge the decision by what you knew, not by what the dice did.*

*An outcome is evidence to examine, not a verdict on the person who faced it.*

*A good decision can lose. A bad decision can win. Only one of them can be repeated on purpose.*

## The written page: on destiny {.unnumbered}

*If your destiny is written, your effort may be part of the ink.*

*Nobody has ever read their destiny in advance. We only read it backward.*

*A caused action can still be a cause.*

*Destiny is a beautiful word for the past and a dangerous word for the future.*

*There is no version of destiny in which the bow draws itself.*

*A story can make the past feel inevitable without making the future predictable.*

*Maktub, it is written, is almost always said in the past tense.*

*The words we use for uncertainty turn, in time, into rules for how we live.*

*Taking responsibility begins with an honest account of the room you have to act.*

*A choice becomes clearer when its constraints are named.*

*Your room may be small. It is almost never zero.*

*Freedom is not only something to believe in. It is a room you can make larger.*

## The breath: on prayer and hope {.unnumbered}

*Prayer is what effort sounds like when it reaches the edge of its power.*

*Every people that learned to pray also invented a proverb about working.*

*An unanswered prayer is not a verdict on the one who prayed.*

*Tie the camel. Then trust.*

*Fantasy rehearses the arrival. Hope rehearses the road.*

*Give your hope more than one road to travel.*

*Hope becomes practical when it can name a next step.*

*Hope is not a prediction. It is a direction.*

*Hope that sets a date with the universe will one day be stood up.*

*A deadline is a tool. A prophecy is a trap.*

*Hold faith in the end of the story, and honesty about this chapter.*

## The archer: on living with all four {.unnumbered}

*A rejection tells you that one door closed. It does not map every door you can reach.*

*Let feedback change your approach before rejection changes your estimate of your worth.*

*You are usually stronger than your forecast.*

*Do not invent an explanation for what remains unknown.*

*In love, the target is also an archer.*

*Another person's freedom is part of the reality love must respect.*

*A shared future cannot be built from one person's certainty alone.*

*Some losses take away a future we had already begun to live in our minds.*

*You do not have to call a loss a gift to build a life after it.*

*Hope should leave room for a future you have not yet imagined.*

*Before you call persistence a virtue, ask what it is preserving.*

*A hard road and a closed road need two different kinds of courage.*

*Changing direction can protect the value that first made you begin.*

*Quit the road if you must. Keep the reason.*

*The next useful step can be clear even while the final outcome remains uncertain.*

*A plan becomes more honest when it includes what you will do if it fails.*

*Small records can correct the large stories disappointment tells us.*

*After the arrow leaves, the archer remains.*

*The arrow that misses is a loss. The arrow never released becomes a question you carry.*

*You can give a goal your best without giving it your entire worth.*

*The result belongs in your story. It does not have to become the title of your life.*

*A life can remain unfinished in its ambitions and still be full in its meaning.*

*In the end, it does matter. Not because of where the arrow landed, but because of who drew the bow.*
