# A small request {.unnumbered}

If this book helped you, even a little, there is one thing you could do that would help it reach someone else who needs it. Leave an honest review wherever you bought it, or pass it to a friend who is standing in their own field right now, looking at the place where their arrow should have landed.

Most people who are struggling with these questions do not go looking for a book about them. They find one because somebody they trust put it in their hands.

# About the author {.unnumbered}

Muhammad Waqas has spent years thinking about four ideas that people build their lives on, hard work, luck, destiny and hope, and about why they so often seem to contradict each other. He noticed, many times, that when he worked hard his luck seemed to improve. He also watched hardworking, hopeful people lose the things they wanted most. *The Archer and the Wind* is his attempt to make sense of both.
